# Code of conduct

Every repository in the PCFHub organisation, and every space around it — issues,
pull requests, discussions and the hub itself — follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be considerate, assume good faith, criticise work rather than people,
and make room for someone asking their first question. Harassment, personal
attacks and publishing someone's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks it through the contact form at
<https://pcfhub.dev/contact>, saying which repository or page it happened in.
Reports are read by the maintainers of the organisation and kept confidential.
A maintainer who is the subject of a report takes no part in handling it.

The maintainers decide what to do as the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe, from a private word to a permanent ban.
